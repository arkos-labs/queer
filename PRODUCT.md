# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TailwindCSS

## Users

Queer individuals looking for a safe space to connect, communicate, and browse directories of safe places/people. Mobile and desktop users.

## Product Purpose

To connect the queer community through real-time messaging, directory browsing, and notifications, providing a cohesive, native-feeling app experience on the web.

## Positioning

A dedicated, safe, and curated community platform specifically for the queer community, with real-time features and a premium native-like interface.

## Operating Context

Used both on desktop and mobile. Fast interactions, real-time messaging, and profile browsing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Supabase for Auth, Realtime Postgres, and Storage. React Router for navigation. 

## Brand Commitments

The visual identity is now "Impeccable" (Neo Kinpaku), utilizing dark lacquer backgrounds, kinpaku gold, and verdigris patina.
